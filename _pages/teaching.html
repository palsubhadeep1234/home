Connecting physical intuition, mathematical models, and computational investigation.

Teaching approach

My aim is to help students move between a physical question, a mathematical description, and a computational test. In mechanics and materials courses, I would use simulation exercises to make assumptions visible, compare predictions with reference solutions or experiments, and ask students to explain where a model applies.

My teaching experience includes graduate courses in atomistic simulation, finite element methods, and structural dynamics, with responsibilities spanning instruction, office hours, assessment, and project guidance.
