## The scientific question

Can AI support materials discovery while making the evidence, assumptions, and unresolved questions behind a recommendation explicit? My current postdoctoral work investigates structured reasoning and coordinated scientific agents.

## Current research

### Graph-native scientific reasoning

I co-developed Graph-PRefLexOR, which organizes hypothesis generation into explicit stages of mechanism exploration, relation mapping, graph construction, and synthesis. My contributions include a 100-question materials-science benchmark and analyses of reasoning representations and semantic diversity.

[Graph-native reinforcement learning · AI and Science, in press](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44486-026-00008-0)

### Collective and decentralized discovery

In ScienceClaw + Infinite, I developed a polymer-physics skill module and an elastomer-design workflow connecting theoretical models and molecular-simulation-derived surrogates. In SwarmWorld, I conducted computational experiments and analyses of how shared environments and persistent artifacts shape specialization and collective discovery.

- [ScienceClaw + Infinite · preprint, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14312)
- [SwarmWorld · preprint, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26081)

### Knowledge-grounded material substitution

In collaboration with Saint-Gobain, I co-developed a hierarchical multi-agent approach connecting material requirements, literature evidence, available-material information, and processing constraints. The work proposed a PFAS-substitute multilayer candidate and a laboratory-scale processing route; the proposal is distinct from an experimentally established replacement.

[Hierarchical multi-agent material substitution · preprint, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14123)

### Scientific AI for cementitious systems

My ongoing work also connects literature-derived knowledge graphs with multi-agent reasoning for 3D-printed cementitious materials, linking rheology, microstructure, and printability to testable hypotheses and experimental investigation.
